How It Works

  1. Complete the seller application form below.
  2. Wait for your application to be reviewed and approved.
  3. After approval, use your Seller Dashboard to add products.
  4. Manage customer orders through your dashboard.

Our Commission

Edesvino charges a 5% commission on eligible sales. The seller's share is 95% of the eligible sale amount.

Example: On an eligible sale of ₦10,000, Edesvino's commission is ₦500 and the seller's share is ₦9,500.

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.
