How Selling Works

  1. Complete the seller application form.
  2. Wait for Edesvino to review your application.
  3. After approval, access your seller dashboard.
  4. Add your products with accurate prices and descriptions.
  5. Manage orders and follow the marketplace's order process.

Simple, Transparent Commission

Edesvino's commission is 5% on eligible sales.

For an eligible sale of ₦10,000:

  • Edesvino commission: ₦500
  • Seller's share: ₦9,500

This example assumes the full ₦10,000 is eligible for commission and excludes any other applicable fees, refunds, or adjustments.
